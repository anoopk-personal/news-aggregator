## AI News Digest

### AI Agents, Frameworks, and Developer Tools
**Stanford Releases Open-Source, On-Device Agent Framework 'OpenJarvis'**
Stanford researchers have launched OpenJarvis, an open-source framework for building personal AI agents that run entirely on-device. The project aims to provide a local-first infrastructure for AI systems, complete with tools, memory, and learning capabilities, moving beyond just model execution to create a full software stack for private, personal AI. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**AWS Launches Strands Labs for Experimental AI Agent Projects**
Amazon Web Services has introduced Strands Labs, a new GitHub organization dedicated to hosting experimental projects related to agent-based AI development. This move signals AWS's increased investment and focus on the rapidly growing AI agent ecosystem, providing a space for developers to explore new agentic AI concepts. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Perplexity Expands API to Include Agentic Workflows**
Perplexity has announced an expansion of its API, giving developers access to its agentic workflows and orchestration capabilities. This allows third-party developers to build applications on top of Perplexity's advanced, web-connected search and answer-generation engine, moving beyond simple Q&amp;A to more complex, multi-step tasks. (Source: The New Stack)

**Gumloop Raises $50M to Empower Employees as AI Agent Builders**
AI startup Gumloop has secured $50 million in funding led by Benchmark to develop an intuitive agent-building platform for enterprise employees. The investment highlights a major trend toward democratizing AI, enabling non-technical workers to create their own AI agents for automating business tasks without needing to code. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**'gstack' Open-Sourced as a Claude-Based Coding System**
Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan has released gstack, an open-source toolkit that structures AI-assisted coding using Anthropic's Claude model. The system separates the software development process into eight distinct modes—including planning, code review, and QA—to improve the reliability and quality of AI-generated code. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**DeepMind's 'Aletheia' Agent Tackles Professional Mathematical Research**
Google DeepMind introduced Aletheia, an AI agent designed to advance from solving competition-level math problems to conducting professional research. Aletheia works by iteratively generating, verifying, and revising long-horizon proofs in natural language, demonstrating the potential for AI to contribute to novel scientific discovery. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**NanoClaw and Docker Partner for Secure AI Agent Sandboxing**
The open-source agent project NanoClaw has entered a partnership with Docker to isolate AI agents inside secure MicroVM sandboxes. This collaboration directly addresses significant security concerns about autonomous agents by providing a contained, lightweight environment for their execution, preventing them from accessing the host system. (Source: The New Stack, AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**Startup 'Nyne' Raises $5.3M to Give AI Agents Human Context**
Founded by a father-son duo, the startup Nyne has raised $5.3 million in seed funding to build data infrastructure that provides AI agents with missing human context. The platform aims to make agents more effective and personalized by giving them secure access to a user's information and preferences. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**NVIDIA Introduces 'NeMo Retriever' for Advanced Agentic Retrieval**
NVIDIA has launched the NeMo Retriever, a new pipeline designed to go beyond simple semantic similarity for information retrieval. This "agentic retrieval" system allows AI agents to perform more complex, multi-step searches, improving the quality and relevance of information provided to large language models. (Source: Hugging Face - Blog)

**Analysis Predicts AI Agents Will Replace 35% of SaaS Tools by 2030**
A market analysis suggests a major shift in the software industry, with AI agents projected to replace or absorb 35% of single-purpose SaaS products by 2030, according to Gartner and Deloitte projections. The trend reflects a move from buying software access per seat to purchasing autonomous outcomes delivered by agents. (Source: Towards AI - Medium)

### New Models, Hardware, and Infrastructure
**NVIDIA Unveils 120B Parameter 'Nemotron 3 Super' Open-Source Model**
NVIDIA has released Nemotron 3 Super, a 120 billion parameter open-source model specifically engineered for complex, agentic AI applications. The model features a hybrid Mamba-Attention Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture, which reportedly delivers 5x higher throughput than comparable models, closing the gap with proprietary systems. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6 Adds Adaptive Reasoning and Context Compaction**
The latest version of Anthropic's model, Claude Opus 4.6, introduces "Adaptive Thinking" and a "Compaction API" to address context degradation in long-running agent tasks. While it boasts a 1 million token context window, independent tests revealed a 49% detection rate for binary backdoors, raising security concerns alongside its performance claims. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Nvidia to Invest $26 Billion in Building Open-Weight AI Models**
Regulatory filings have revealed that Nvidia plans to spend $26 billion to develop its own open-weight AI models. This massive investment signals a strategic move for the chipmaker to compete directly with leading AI labs like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, expanding its role from hardware provider to a vertically integrated AI powerhouse. (Source: Wired)

**Meta Develops Four New Custom Chips for AI and Recommendation Systems**
Meta announced it is developing four new custom processors, part of its MTIA (Meta Training and Inference Accelerator) family. This initiative is the company's latest attempt to build its own AI hardware, aiming to reduce its multi-billion dollar dependence on industry leader Nvidia and optimize performance for its specific workloads. (Source: Wired)

**Anthropic's Claude Gains Interactive Data Visualization Capabilities**
Anthropic has updated its Claude model to allow it to generate interactive charts and diagrams directly within the chat interface. This new feature adds a powerful data visualization tool, enabling users to better understand and explore data by interacting with graphs, maps, and other visuals created by the AI. (Source: The New Stack)

## CRICKET News Digest

### The Hundred Auction Makes Headlines
**Record spending and landmark signings at inaugural auction**
The first-ever Hundred auction saw London Spirit sign uncapped 21-year-old all-rounder James Coles for a record £390,000. In a significant move, the Indian-owned Sunrisers Leeds franchise bought Pakistan spinner Abrar Ahmed, allaying fears that Pakistani players would be ignored. Other major buys included Aiden Markram to MSG and Jordan Cox to Welsh Fire for £300,000. (Source: BBC Sport, ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Women's auction hailed as a milestone for the game**
The women's auction created some of the UK's highest-paid female athletes, with Sophie Devine fetching £210,000 from Welsh Fire. All-rounders Danielle Gibson and Issy Wong also secured deals over £100,000, a moment described as a huge step forward for women's professional cricket and its visibility. (Source: BBC Sport, ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### Pakistan and Bangladesh ODI Series
**Pakistan level series amid controversy and injury**
Pakistan squared the ODI series 1-1 by bowling Bangladesh out for just 114 in a rain-affected chase of 242, thanks to an all-round effort from Sadaqat Ali. The match was marked by a controversial run-out of Agha Salman by Mehidy Hasan Miraz, which sparked debate over sportsmanship, while Pakistan's Hussain Talat was stretchered off with a shoulder injury. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com, BBC Sport)

### Australian Domestic Scene Heats Up
**Bowlers dominate as Sheffield Shield final race intensifies**
The race for the Sheffield Shield final is going down to the wire, with strong bowling performances defining the latest round. Queensland's Michael Neser took 5-37 against Tasmania, and WA's Corey Rocchiccioli claimed a five-wicket haul against NSW to keep their teams' hopes alive. In the batting department, Henry Hunt's 81 helped South Australia in their push for a final spot against Victoria. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### International Tours &amp; T20 Leagues
**New-look teams and postponed series shape international calendar**
Just ten days after their T20 World Cup semi-final, new-look New Zealand and South Africa squads are beginning a five-match T20I series to audition talent for the 2028 tournament. Elsewhere, West Indies named an unchanged squad for their home T20Is against Australia, while Afghanistan's series against Sri Lanka in the UAE was postponed due to regional conflict. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com, BBC Sport)

**Player movements impact Pakistan Super League**
The upcoming Pakistan Super League (PSL) has seen several roster changes, with Blessing Muzarabani pulling out after securing an IPL deal with KKR and Gudakesh Motie also withdrawing. However, New Zealand coaches Luke Ronchi and Jacob Oram have been cleared to fulfill their coaching roles with Islamabad United despite a clash with national team duties. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### Player Awards and Career Updates
**BCCI honours Indian cricket legends and current stars**
The BCCI will confer Lifetime Achievement awards upon former captain and coach Rahul Dravid, women's cricket icon Mithali Raj, and 1983 World Cup winner Roger Binny. For the 2024-25 season, Shubman Gill and Smriti Mandhana were named India's best international cricketers in the men's and women's categories, respectively. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Mark Wood reflects on future amid injury recovery**
England fast bowler Mark Wood confirmed his injured knee is improving but admitted that he has started thinking about life after cricket for the first time. The update provides insight into the mindset of a key player managing his long-term fitness and career longevity. (Source: BBC Sport)
