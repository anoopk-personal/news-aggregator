The Model Context Protocol (MCP), once seen as a universal standard for agent tool use, is now facing scrutiny. As autonomous AI agents become more common in corporate networks, experts are calling for "interaction infrastructure" to prevent automation waste and chaos. (Source: [AI News](https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/why-ai-agents-need-interaction-infrastructure/))
