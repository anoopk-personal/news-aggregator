## AI News Digest

### Major Model &amp; Platform Releases

**OpenAI Unveils GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark for Real-Time Coding**
OpenAI has released a research preview of GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark, a new model optimized for extreme speed in code generation. Achieved through a deep hardware-software collaboration with chipmaker Cerebras, the model is reportedly 15 times faster than its predecessor, capable of generating over 1000 tokens per second. This focus on near-instant response times marks a significant step towards real-time, conversational coding assistants. (Source: OpenAI News, MarkTechPost, Ars Technica, The New Stack)

**Google Launches Gemini 3 Deep Think for Advanced Scientific Reasoning**
Google has announced a major update to its Gemini model family with Gemini 3 Deep Think, specifically engineered to accelerate science, research, and engineering. The model introduces a "reasoning mode" with internal verification to solve complex problems and achieved a breakthrough score of 84.6% on the ARC-AGI-2 benchmark. This release signals a strategic pivot towards AI systems capable of autonomous scientific discovery. (Source: AI, MarkTechPost)

**DeepMind Introduces Aletheia, an AI Agent for Mathematical Research**
Google DeepMind has unveiled Aletheia, a specialized AI agent designed to advance from solving competition-level math problems to conducting professional mathematical research. Building on the success of models at the International Mathematical Olympiad, Aletheia can navigate vast scientific literature and construct long-horizon proofs by iteratively generating, verifying, and revising solutions in natural language. This represents a move towards fully autonomous research agents. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Alibaba Enters Physical AI Race with Open-Source Robot Model RynnBrain**
Chinese tech giant Alibaba has released RynnBrain, an open-source multimodal model designed to power physical robots. The model aims to provide robots with the ability to perceive their environment and execute complex physical tasks. The move signals China's growing focus on "physical AI" to address labor shortages and compete in the global robotics market. (Source: AI News)

**AI Rivals Including Google and OpenAI Back Paris Startup Accelerator**

**AI Inference Startup Modal Labs in Talks to Raise at $2.5B Valuation**
Modal Labs, a startup specializing in AI inference infrastructure, is reportedly in talks to raise a new funding round at a $2.5 billion valuation, with General Catalyst expected to lead. ### The Rise of Agentic AI in the Enterprise

**Agentic AI Delivers Superior ROI in Finance and Insurance**
New reports indicate that agentic AI, which uses autonomous agents to perform complex tasks, is driving significant returns on investment, particularly in back-office functions. In accounts payable, agentic AI delivered an average ROI of 80%, compared to 67% for general AI projects, by turning manual processes into autonomous workflows. Similarly, insurance leaders are using agentic systems to cut operational costs and scale efficiency beyond pilot programs. (Source: AI News)

**Didero Lands $30M to Automate Manufacturing Procurement with AI Agents**
Startup Didero has raised $30 million to expand its "agentic autopilot" for manufacturing procurement. The company's AI acts as a coordinating layer on top of a company's existing Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software, automatically reading communications and executing tasks like updating orders and managing suppliers. (Source: TechCrunch)

**Spotify Claims Top Developers Now Supervise AI Instead of Writing Code**
Spotify revealed a dramatic shift in its development process, stating its "best developers haven't written a line of code since December" due to the adoption of AI tools. The company credits Anthropic's Claude Code and its internal AI system, "Honk," with massively accelerating development cycles, allowing senior engineers to focus on system design and supervision rather than manual coding. (Source: TechCrunch)

**UK Ministry of Defence Taps Red Hat for Unified AI Platform**
The UK Ministry of Defence (MOD) has selected Red Hat to architect a unified AI and hybrid cloud backbone across its entire organization. The strategic agreement aims to break down data silos and create a consistent platform for developing and deploying AI models from the data center to the tactical edge, enhancing operational capabilities. (Source: AI News)

**Barclays Bets on AI to Cut Costs and Boost Returns**
Following a 12% jump in annual profit to £9.1 billion, Barclays announced a strategic plan that heavily relies on AI to cut costs and improve efficiency. The bank raised its performance targets, aiming for a return on tangible equity (RoTE) of over 14% by 2028, with AI-driven automation being a key pillar of the strategy. (Source: AI News)

### AI in Science, Health, and Research

**MIT Launches "AI Evidence" Initiative to Fight Poverty**
MIT's Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL) has launched "Project AI Evidence," a new research and policy initiative. The project will connect governments, NGOs, and tech companies with world-class economists to rigorously test, evaluate, and scale AI-powered innovations aimed at alleviating global poverty. (Source: MIT News)

**AI and Synthetic Biology Combined to Combat Antimicrobial Resistance**
Researchers are leveraging a combination of AI and synthetic biology to address the growing global threat of drug-resistant infections. As the development of new antibiotics has slowed, AI is being used to accelerate the discovery of novel antibacterial tools and mechanisms to fight superbugs. (Source: MIT News)

**New AI Algorithm Creates Detailed Maps of Brain's White Matter**
An AI algorithm developed at MIT provides a new window into the brainstem by reliably resolving distinct nerve bundles (white matter pathways) in live diffusion MRI scans. This tool allows for finer tracking of these pathways than ever before, enabling researchers and clinicians to better detect signs of injury or diseases like Parkinson's. (Source: MIT News)

**AI Forecasting Model Aims to Improve NHS Resource Efficiency**
Researchers at the University of Hertfordshire have developed an operational AI forecasting model in partnership with regional NHS bodies. The model applies machine learning to large archives of historical health data to improve forward-looking operational planning and make more efficient use of critical healthcare resources. (Source: AI News)

### Security, Governance, and Ethical Concerns

**Google: State-Sponsored Hackers Are Using AI for Cyberattacks**
A report from Google's Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) revealed that state-sponsored hacking groups from Iran, North Korea, China, and Russia are actively using large language models. The threat actors are leveraging AI to generate scripts, craft sophisticated phishing emails, and research vulnerabilities, accelerating their cyberattack campaigns. (Source: AI News, Ars Technica)

**US Border Patrol Signs Deal with Clearview AI for Facial Recognition**
US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has signed a contract to provide its intelligence units with access to Clearview AI's controversial facial recognition technology. The tool, built on a database of billions of images scraped from the public internet, will be used for "tactical targeting," raising significant privacy and civil liberties concerns. (Source: Wired)

**Meta Reportedly Plans to Add Facial Recognition to Smart Glasses**
A report claims Meta is developing a feature internally known as "Name Tag" for its Ray-Ban smart glasses. The feature would use the glasses' camera and an AI assistant to identify people in the wearer's field of view and provide information about them, a move that would represent a major escalation in public-facing facial recognition technology. (Source: TechCrunch)

**Newsweek CEO Warns Publishers to Adapt to AI as the New News Gateway**
Dev Pragad, CEO of Newsweek, has issued a warning to media leaders, stating that they must fundamentally adapt their strategies as AI platforms become the primary mediators of news. He argues that as AI-driven search and conversational interfaces increasingly influence how audiences discover information, the traditional relationship between publishers and the public is being irrevocably changed. (Source: AI News)

**Attackers Attempt to "Clone" Google's Gemini Model via Prompting**
Google reported that it detected and shut down a coordinated effort by attackers to "distill" its Gemini model. The attackers issued over 100,000 prompts to the model in an attempt to mimic its capabilities and create a clone at a fraction of the development cost, highlighting a new type of threat against proprietary AI systems. (Source: Ars Technica)

### Developer Tools &amp; Technical Breakthroughs

**NVIDIA Researchers Announce 20x KV Cache Compression for LLMs**
NVIDIA researchers have introduced KVTC (Key-Value Transform Coding), a new pipeline that can compress the memory-intensive KV cache used in LLMs by a factor of 20. This breakthrough can significantly reduce the memory footprint for serving large models, leading to higher throughput, lower latency, and more efficient use of hardware. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**GitHub Releases Copilot SDK to Build Agentic Workflows**
Now in technical preview, the GitHub Copilot SDK allows developers to integrate the powerful engine behind the GitHub Copilot CLI directly into their own applications. This move is designed to make it easier for the developer community to build custom, agentic workflows that can automate complex software development tasks. (Source: InfoQ)

**OpenAI Details How It Scaled PostgreSQL to Power ChatGPT**
In a technical blog, OpenAI explained its architecture for supporting ChatGPT's massive scale, which handles millions of queries per second. The company runs a single-primary PostgreSQL deployment on Azure with nearly 50 read replicas, combined with aggressive query optimization and offloading write-heavy workloads, to maintain low-latency performance for hundreds of millions of users. (Source: InfoQ)

**New Techniques Emerge for Efficient Model Training and Alignment**
Technical tutorials have detailed powerful new methods for building more efficient AI systems. These include using Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to align LLMs with human preferences on a single GPU, and Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL) to create sentence embeddings that can be truncated for ultra-fast retrieval without significant quality loss. (Source: MarkTechPost)

The win is a significant moment for Zimbabwe cricket on the world stage. (Source: BBC Sport)

India showcased their dominance with a massive victory over Namibia, their biggest ever by runs in a T20 World Cup. The UAE defeated Canada by five wickets in a tense chase to keep their slim hopes of qualifying for the Super 8s alive.
