### Major Model and Product Releases

**Anthropic Upgrades Claude with New Model and Design Tool**
Anthropic has released Claude Opus 4.7, an updated frontier model with substantial improvements in agentic coding, high-resolution vision, and the ability to perform long-horizon autonomous tasks. The company also launched Claude Design, a new product in research preview that allows users to generate visuals from text prompts, positioning it as a competitor to tools like Figma and Canva and signaling a push into creative and design software. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/18/anthropic-releases-claude-opus-4-7-a-major-upgrade-for-agentic-coding-high-resolution-vision-and-long-horizon-autonomous-tasks/), [AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/anthropic-launches-claude-design-a-new-product-for-creating-quick-visuals/))

**Google Enhances its AI Ecosystem with Gemma 4 and Chrome AI Mode**
Google has released Gemma 4, a new family of open-weight models under the Apache 2.0 license, with sizes up to 31B parameters, multimodal capabilities, and context windows up to 256K tokens. Concurrently, Google updated its Chrome browser with an "AI Mode" that opens web pages side-by-side with an AI chat interface, aiming to streamline research and reduce tab-hopping. (Source: [InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/04/google-gemm4/), [AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/google-now-lets-you-explore-the-web-side-by-side-with-ai-mode/))

**OpenAI Expands Codex Capabilities Beyond Coding**
OpenAI is significantly upgrading Codex, its AI coding assistant, to give it more control over a user's desktop environment and integrate it more deeply with ChatGPT. This move is seen as a direct challenge to competitors like Anthropic's Claude and a major step towards creating a unified "superapp" that combines chat, coding, and broader desktop automation. (Source: [AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/openai-takes-aim-at-anthropic-with-beefed-up-codex-that-gives-it-more-power-over-your-desktop/), [Artificial Intelligence News, Analysis and Resources - The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openais-superapp-takes-shape/))

**New Open-Source and Specialized Models Enter the Fray**
The Qwen Team open-sourced Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) vision-language model with only 3B active parameters, which has shown strong performance in coding benchmarks against larger models. Separately, NVIDIA released Ising, its first open quantum AI model family designed for hybrid quantum-classical systems, aiming to bridge the gap between quantum research and real-world applications. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/16/qwen-team-open-sources-qwen3-6-35b-a3b-a-sparse-moe-vision-language-model-with-3b-active-parameters-and-agentic-coding-capabilities/), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/19/nvidia-releases-ising/))

**xAI and Physical Intelligence Launch New APIs and Robot Brain**
Elon Musk's xAI has launched standalone Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech APIs, built on the same infrastructure that powers Grok Voice, targeting the enterprise voice developer market. Meanwhile, robotics startup Physical Intelligence unveiled π0.7, a new robot "brain" model that can reportedly figure out tasks it was never explicitly taught, marking a step toward general-purpose robotics. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/18/xai-launches-standalone-grok-speech-to-text-and-text-to-speech-apis-targeting-enterprise-voice-developers/), [AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/physical-intelligence-a-hot-robotics-startup-says-its-new-robot-brain-can-figure-out-tasks-it-was-never-taught/))

**Google Advances Agentic Math Research with Aletheia**
Google's AI system, Aletheia, which uses the Gemini 3 Deep Think model, has demonstrated significant progress in autonomous mathematical research. The system successfully solved 6 out of 10 novel problems in the FirstProof challenge and achieved a high score on the IMO-ProofBench, showcasing its ability to discover research-level proofs without human intervention.
