**NVIDIA Unveils Star Elastic: Three Models in a Single Checkpoint**
NVIDIA researchers introduced Star Elastic, a post-training method that embeds 30B, 23B, and 12B parameter reasoning models within a single checkpoint. The technique also enables "elastic budget control" during inference, which can improve accuracy by up to 16% and lower latency by 1.9x compared to standard methods.
(Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/09/nvidia-ai-releases-star-elastic-one-checkpoint-that-contains-30b-23b-and-12b-reasoning-models-with-zero-shot-slicing/))

OpenAI has shipped a Chrome extension for its Codex AI agent, enabling it to perform tasks directly within the browser on macOS and Windows. The extension represents a significant step toward creating AI agents that can operate across a user's digital environment.
(Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/08/openai-adds-chrome-extension-to-codex-letting-its-ai-agent-access-linkedin-salesforce-gmail-and-internal-tools-via-signed-in-sessions/))

### New Research &amp; Techniques

**Anthropic Creates Autoencoders to Explain Claude's "Thinking"**
Anthropic has developed natural language autoencoders that can convert the internal numerical "activations" of its Claude model directly into human-readable text. This breakthrough in AI interpretability provides an unprecedented window into how the model processes information and generates responses. By making the model's internal state legible, researchers can better understand, debug, and improve its reasoning capabilities.
(Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/08/anthropic-introduces-natural-language-autoencoders-that-convert-claudes-internal-activations-directly-into-human-readable-text-explanations/))

**Google's TurboQuant Research Promises 6x Memory Savings for LLMs**
A Google paper presented at ICLR 2026 introduced TurboQuant, an algorithm that compresses an LLM's key-value (KV) cache down to 3 bits with minimal loss in quality. The KV cache is a major consumer of GPU memory during inference, and this technique can provide up to a 6x reduction in memory usage. This could significantly lower the cost and hardware requirements for self-hosting large language models.
(Source: [Towards AI - Medium](https://pub.towardsai.net/is-3-bit-kv-cache-the-holy-grail-a-reality-check-on-googles-turboquant-e1ac22daa253?source=rss----98111c9905da---4))

**Allen Institute's EMO Aims for Emergent Modularity in MoE Models**
Researchers at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2) have introduced EMO, a pretraining method for Mixture of Experts (MoE) models. The technique is designed to encourage emergent modularity, where different "experts" within the model naturally specialize in distinct tasks or concepts. This research could lead to more efficient, specialized, and interpretable AI systems that are easier to adapt and scale.
(Source: [Hugging Face - Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/emo))

**OncoAgent Framework Proposed for Privacy-Preserving Cancer Care**
A new research paper details OncoAgent, a dual-tier, multi-agent framework designed for privacy-preserving clinical decision support in oncology. The system demonstrates a potential application for complex AI agent systems in highly sensitive fields like healthcare. Its architecture is built to handle complex medical data while maintaining patient privacy, a key hurdle for AI adoption in medicine.
(Source: [Hugging Face - Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/lablab-ai-amd-developer-hackathon/oncoagent-official-paper))
