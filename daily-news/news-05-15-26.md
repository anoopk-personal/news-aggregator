### AI Models and Performance
**Nous Research Accelerates LLM Pre-Training with Token Superposition Training**
Nous Research has introduced Token Superposition Training (TST), a two-phase pre-training method that can speed up LLM pre-training by up to 2.5x. This technique averages contiguous token embeddings during an initial phase, then reverts to standard next-token prediction, without altering model architecture or inference behavior. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/13/nous-research-releases-token-superposition-training-to-speed-up-llm-pre-training-by-up-to-2-5x-across-270m-to-10b-parameter-models/))

**Fastino Labs Open-Sources GLiGuard, a Compact Safety Moderation Model**
Fastino Labs has released GLiGuard, a 300M parameter open-source safety moderation model that matches or exceeds the accuracy of models 23-90 times its size. GLiGuard evaluates prompt safety, jailbreak detection, harm classification, and refusal detection in a single pass, offering up to 16x higher throughput and 16.6x lower latency. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/13/fastino-labs-open-sources-gliguard-a-300m-parameter-safety-moderation-model-that-matches-or-exceeds-accuracy-of-models-23-90x-its-size/))

**Supertone Releases Supertonic v3 Text-to-Speech Model**
Seoul-based speech AI company Supertone has launched Supertonic v3, its third-generation on-device text-to-speech engine. This version supports 31 languages, features improved reading stability, and includes expressive tags, all while maintaining the same inference contract for existing integrations. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/15/supertone-releases-supertonic-v3-on-device-text-to-speech-model-with-31-language-support-fewer-reading-failures-and-expression-tags/))

**IBM Releases Granite Embedding Multilingual R2**
IBM has released Granite Embedding Multilingual R2, an open Apache 2.0 licensed multilingual embedding model with a 32K context window. This model is touted as offering the best sub-100M retrieval quality, making it a significant contribution to open-source multilingual AI. (Source: [Hugging Face - Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-granite/granite-embedding-multilingual-r2))
