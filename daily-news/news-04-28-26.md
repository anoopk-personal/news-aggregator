**LeCun's Research Focuses on "Geodesics" to Move Beyond LLMs**
Yann LeCun's new research on "world models" is based on the idea that true AI understanding requires learning the underlying principles of the world, a concept described as finding "geodesics" (the most efficient path or structure). This approach stands in contrast to current LLMs that simply predict the next "token" (a superficial data point), arguing that a deeper, causal understanding is necessary for advanced reasoning and planning. (Source: [Towards AI - Medium](https://pub.towardsai.net/tokens-are-breadcrumbs-geodesics-are-the-path-lecun-just-proved-the-difference-matters-5c9592b8222b/))

(Source: [Hugging Face - Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/raw2insights-adaptive-ultrasound-imaging))

**Analysis of "Claude Mythos" Reveals High Accuracy and High Exploitability**

### Building with AI: Tools, Techniques, and the Developer Experience

**GitHub Moves Copilot to Usage-Based Billing as AI Costs Rise**
GitHub has confirmed it is overhauling its pricing for the popular Copilot AI coding assistant, shifting from a flat-rate subscription to a usage-based billing model. This change reflects the significant and variable computational costs of running advanced AI models. For developers and companies, this means that costs will now directly correlate with usage, potentially increasing expenses for heavy users and requiring more careful budget management. (Source: [Artificial Intelligence News, Analysis and Resources - The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/github-copilot-usage-billing/))

**Microsoft Executives Warn AI is "Hollowing Out" the Junior Developer Pipeline**
In a recent paper, Microsoft's Mark Russinovich and Scott Hanselman argue that agentic AI is creating an "AI drag" that threatens the junior developer talent pipeline. They contend that AI tools disproportionately boost senior developer productivity while automating entry-level tasks, leading companies to stop hiring junior engineers, a trend supported by a 67% drop in entry-level hiring since 2022. They propose a "preceptor model" borrowed from medical education to ensure the next generation of developers can be trained. (Source: [InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/04/junior-developer-pipeline-crisis/))

**Developers Shift from "Vibe Coding" to "Spec-Driven Development"**
A new methodology called Spec-Driven Development (SDD) is being promoted to create more reliable AI-assisted software. The move is a response to the failures of "Vibe Coding"—relying on AI assistants with simple prompts—which often produces code that is architecturally incompatible with complex projects. SDD emphasizes providing the AI with detailed specifications and architectural context to ensure the generated code is robust and correct. (Source: [Towards AI - Medium](https://pub.towardsai.net/beyond-prompting-spec-driven-development-34e9f40e325a/))

**Executives Embrace "Vibe Coding" to Build Their Own Tools**
A trend of non-technical executives using AI coding assistants to build their own software tools, dubbed "vibe coding," is on the rise. This empowers business leaders to rapidly prototype solutions without traditional development cycles, with one executive noting they were "tired of explaining it to somebody who was supposed to build it for me." To support this, Google and Kaggle have launched a new "AI Agents Vibe Coding Course." (Source: [Artificial Intelligence News, Analysis and Resources - The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/vibe-coding-ceos-executives-ai-agents/) and [AI](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/kaggle-genai-intensive-course-vibe-coding-june-2026/))

**Uber Uses AI to Automate Massive JUnit Test Migration**
Uber engineers successfully migrated over 75,000 test classes from the legacy JUnit 4 framework to the modern JUnit 5 using automated code transformation tools like OpenRewrite.
