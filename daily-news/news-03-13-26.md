## AI News Digest

### AI Agents and Autonomous Systems
**NVIDIA and AWS Launch New Agent Frameworks**
NVIDIA is preparing to launch NemoClaw, an open-source platform for building and evaluating AI agents, which is reportedly already in use by ServiceNow. Concurrently, Amazon Web Services (AWS) has launched Strands Labs, a new GitHub organization to host experimental projects focused on agent-based AI development. These initiatives signal a major push by cloud and hardware giants to provide the core infrastructure for the next wave of autonomous AI systems. (Source: The New Stack, InfoQ)

**Stanford Releases OpenJarvis for On-Device Personal Agents**
Researchers at Stanford's Scaling Intelligence Lab have released OpenJarvis, an open-source framework for building personal AI agents that run entirely on-device. The framework focuses on the complete software stack, including tools, memory, and learning capabilities, aiming to create a local-first alternative to cloud-based agents for enhanced privacy and control. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Perplexity Expands API to Support Agentic Workflows**
Following its recent launch of Perplexity Computer, the company has expanded its API to give developers access to agentic workflows and orchestration. This allows developers to build applications that can perform multi-step reasoning and actions, moving beyond simple question-answering to more complex, automated tasks. (Source: The New Stack)

**OpenAI Details Agent Security and Runtime Environment**
OpenAI published details on how it designs agents to resist prompt injection and social engineering by constraining risky actions and protecting sensitive data. The company also explained its agent runtime architecture, which uses the Responses API, a shell tool, and hosted containers to create secure and scalable agents with access to files and tools. (Source: OpenAI News)

**New Research Explores Advanced Agent Architectures**
Researchers are exploring novel agent designs, including a "Meta-Agent" that can automatically construct and refine task-specific agents from a simple description. Other work focuses on "Streaming Decision Agents" capable of online replanning and adaptation in dynamic environments, highlighting a move towards more responsive and intelligent autonomous systems. (Source: MarkTechPost)

### New AI Models and Development Platforms
**NVIDIA Unveils Nemotron 3 Super, a 120B Parameter Open Model**
NVIDIA has released Nemotron 3 Super, a 120 billion parameter open-source reasoning model designed for complex multi-agent applications. The model features a hybrid Mamba-Attention Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture, which reportedly delivers five times higher throughput than comparable models, aiming to close the gap with proprietary systems. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6 Gains New Reasoning and Visualization Capabilities**
Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.6, introducing "Adaptive Thinking" and a "Compaction API" to improve performance and reduce context rot in long-running agent tasks. The model, which supports a 1M token context window, can now also generate interactive charts and diagrams, enhancing its utility for data analysis and presentation. (Source: InfoQ, The New Stack)

**Google Releases Gemini Embedding 2 for Multimodal RAG**
Google has launched Gemini Embedding 2, a second-generation multimodal embedding model designed for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. Unlike its text-only predecessor, this model can process text, images, video, audio, and documents, addressing challenges in cross-modal retrieval and high-dimensional data storage for developers. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**NVIDIA Releases Data Pipeline for Training Terminal Agents**
To address the data bottleneck in training autonomous agents, NVIDIA AI has released Nemotron-Terminal, a systematic data engineering pipeline. The open-source project provides a framework for generating high-quality training data for LLMs that operate in terminal (command-line) environments, a key step in creating capable coding and system administration agents. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Fish Audio S2 Aims for More Expressive Text-to-Speech**
Fish Audio has released Fish Audio S2, a new text-to-speech (TTS) model focused on providing highly controllable emotional expression. The open architecture framework supports zero-shot voice cloning and granular emotional control with low latency, representing a shift towards more integrated and expressive Large Audio Models (LAMs). (Source: MarkTechPost)

### Enterprise Adoption and Industry Impact
**Finance Sector Deepens AI Integration for Core Operations**
Financial institutions are moving AI from peripheral projects to core workflows. Canadian insurer Manulife is deploying agent-based AI into its business processes, while E.SUN Bank is collaborating with IBM to establish a formal AI governance framework. Separately, financial infrastructure provider SEI is also working with IBM to use agentic AI for operational automation, signaling a broad industry trend. (Source: AI News)

**Manufacturing and Robotics Embrace Humanoids and Digital Twins**
BMW Group has deployed wheeled humanoid robots from Hexagon Robotics at its Leipzig plant in Germany, a first for the automaker. In a related trend, ABB and NVIDIA are partnering to use physical AI simulation and digital twins to de-risk and optimize factory automation, boosting ROI by solving production issues before physical deployment. (Source: AI News)

**E-commerce and Customer Service Giants Deploy OpenAI Models**
Online furniture retailer Wayfair is using OpenAI models to enhance product catalog accuracy and automate customer support ticket triage. Similarly, Japanese e-commerce giant Rakuten is using OpenAI's Codex to help developers fix software issues twice as fast, reducing mean time to resolution (MTTR) by 50%. (Source: OpenAI News)

**Zendesk Acquires Agentic AI Startup Forethought**
Customer service giant Zendesk has acquired Forethought, a startup specializing in agentic AI for customer support that won the TechCrunch Battlefield competition in 2018. The acquisition highlights the growing importance of autonomous AI agents in the customer service industry to automate and improve support workflows. (Source: TechCrunch)

**FIFA to Run 2026 World Cup Operations on AI**
FIFA is rebuilding its global football operations using AI, with the 48-team 2026 World Cup serving as the first major test. The organization is taking direct control of logistics, a departure from previous reliance on local committees, and will use AI to manage the complexity of the tournament spread across Canada, Mexico, and the United States. (Source: AI News)

**Ford Pro Launches AI Assistant for Fleet Management**
Ford has introduced Ford Pro AI, a new assistant for its U.S.-based Pro telematics subscribers. The AI tool will help fleet owners monitor vehicle data and driver behavior, including whether seatbelts are being used, to improve safety and operational efficiency. (Source: TechCrunch)

**Atlassian Lays Off 1,600 Staff to Reallocate Funds to AI**
Software company Atlassian has laid off 10% of its workforce, affecting approximately 1,600 employees. The company stated the restructuring is intended to funnel more resources and investment into its artificial intelligence initiatives, following a similar move by Block. (Source: TechCrunch)

### AI in Science, Health, and Environment
**Google AI Predicts Flash Floods Using Historical News Reports**
Google AI has developed "Groundsource," a methodology that uses the Gemini model to analyze unstructured public news reports and create structured historical data on natural disasters. Its first application is an open-source dataset of 2.6 million historical urban flash flood events, designed to improve prediction models where official data is scarce. (Source: MarkTechPost, TechCrunch)

**MIT Researchers Develop AI to Forecast Heart Failure and Tumor Progression**
Researchers at MIT, Mass General Brigham, and Harvard developed a deep-learning model that can predict if a heart-failure patient's condition will worsen within a year. In a separate project, another MIT team is building predictive models to decode molecular processes and anticipate how tumors evolve to resist treatment. (Source: MIT News)

## CRICKET News Digest

### The Hundred Auction
**Record-Breaking Bids in Men's and Women's Auctions**
The first-ever Hundred auction saw record-breaking sums, with all-rounder James Coles becoming the most expensive player after being bought by London Spirit for £390,000. The inaugural women's auction was also historic, with Beth Mooney and Sophie Devine earning top paydays of £210,000, making them some of the highest-paid female athletes in the UK. (Source: BBC Sport)

**Abrar Ahmed's Signing Eases Political Tensions**
Sunrisers Leeds, a franchise with Indian ownership, signed Pakistan spinner Abrar Ahmed. The move was seen as a significant step in allaying concerns that Pakistani players would be overlooked by teams affiliated with the Indian Premier League, with commentators calling it a "step in the right direction". (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Concerns Over Lack of Female Head Coaches**
Despite the landmark women's auction, MI London's Lisa Keightley expressed disappointment at the lack of female representation in top coaching roles. Keightley was the only woman serving as a head coach among the eight Hundred teams, highlighting a significant diversity issue in the competition's leadership. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### International Cricket
**Bangladesh Dominates Pakistan in ODI Series**
Bangladesh secured a commanding victory in the opening ODI against Pakistan, bowling the visitors out for just 114, thanks to a five-wicket haul from debutant Nahid Rana. Pakistan, who had fielded four debutants, brought in pacer Haris Rauf for the second ODI but continued to struggle against a dominant Bangladesh side. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**West Indies Name Unchanged Squad for Australia T20Is**
West Indies has announced an unchanged squad for its upcoming home T20I series against Australia. The series will be the first time the two nations have faced each other in an international match since October 2023, providing a key test for both teams. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Afghanistan vs. Sri Lanka Series Postponed**
The upcoming series between Afghanistan and Sri Lanka, which was scheduled to be played in the United Arab Emirates, has been postponed. The decision was made to delay the series until the end of the year due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East. (Source: BBC Sport)

### Domestic Cricket
**New South Wales Wins Dramatic One-Day Cup Final**
New South Wales clinched the Australian One-Day Cup title in a rain-affected final against Tasmania. Guided by Kurtis Patterson, NSW chased down their target in a match reduced to 22 overs per side, which was just 12 minutes away from a washout that would have handed Tasmania the trophy. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Race for Sheffield Shield Final Intensifies**
The final round of the Sheffield Shield will see South Australia and Queensland battle to join Victoria in the final. In a boost for Western Australia, all-rounder Cameron Green is set to play in their last match, while Queensland will have paceman Xavier Bartlett available for their crucial fixture. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### League &amp; Player News

The IPL 2026 season is scheduled to start on March 28 with a match between RCB and SRH in Bengaluru, pending a final stadium inspection. In the PSL, Lahore Qalandars will replace Gudakesh Motie with Dunith Wellalage, while New Zealand coaches Luke Ronchi and Jacob Oram will fulfill their PSL roles despite a clash with national team duties.
