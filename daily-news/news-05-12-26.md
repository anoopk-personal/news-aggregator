Researchers at Tilde Research have introduced Aurora, a new leverage-aware optimizer designed to fix a critical flaw in the widely used Muon optimizer. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/12/tilde-research-introduces-aurora-a-leverage-aware-optimizer-that-fixes-a-hidden-neuron-death-problem-in-muon/))

**Sakana AI and Nvidia Boost LLM Speed with TwELL**
Sakana AI and NVIDIA researchers have introduced TwELL, a method that uses L1 regularization to induce over 99% sparsity in LLM feedforward layers. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/11/sakana-ai-and-nvidia-introduce-twell-with-cuda-kernels-for-20-5-inference-and-21-9-training-speedup-in-llms/))
