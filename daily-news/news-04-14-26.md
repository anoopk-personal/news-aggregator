## AI News Digest

### New Models &amp; Research Breakthroughs

**NVIDIA and UMD Release Open-Source Audio Model**
NVIDIA and the University of Maryland have released Audio Flamingo Next (AF-Next), a powerful, open-source large audio-language model. This is significant as robust, open models that can reason over speech, music, and environmental sounds have lagged behind vision-based AI, and AF-Next aims to bridge that gap in multimodal understanding. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**MiniMax Open-Sources Self-Evolving Agent Model M2.7**
AI research company MiniMax has open-sourced its MiniMax M2.7 model, making the weights publicly available on Hugging Face. The model is notable for being the company's first to actively participate in its own development cycle and has achieved strong benchmark scores, including 56.22% on SWE-Pro. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Google Launches Gemma 4 for On-Device AI**
Google has released Gemma 4, a family of models designed to enable local, on-device AI inference, particularly for Android development. The release aims to support the entire software lifecycle, from coding to production, by bringing powerful agentic AI capabilities directly to edge devices. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Anthropic Previews Claude Mythos with Limited Access**
Anthropic introduced Claude Mythos Preview, its most advanced model yet, showing significant improvements in reasoning, coding, and cybersecurity. Unlike previous releases, public access is withheld; the model is available only to a consortium of tech companies via Project Glasswing after internal tests showed it could effectively discover critical security flaws. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Meta and KAUST Propose "Neural Computers"**
Researchers from Meta AI and KAUST have introduced the concept of Neural Computers (NCs), a new machine architecture where the neural network itself acts as the computer. This paradigm-shifting idea folds computation, memory, and I/O into a single learned model, fundamentally rethinking the relationship between hardware and software. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Google AI Proposes "Vantage" to Measure Durable Skills**
Google AI Research has proposed Vantage, an LLM-based protocol designed to measure so-called "durable skills" like collaboration, creativity, and critical thinking. This aims to address a major gap left by standardized tests, which cannot reliably assess these crucial abilities. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Anthropic's Claude Dominates Conversation at HumanX Conference**
Anthropic's Claude model was reportedly the star of the AI-centric HumanX conference in San Francisco. The widespread discussion and interest in Claude highlight its growing prominence and adoption within the developer and business communities. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)
