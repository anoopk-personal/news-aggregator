Anthropic has released Claude Opus 4.7, a significant upgrade focused on improving agentic coding capabilities, high-resolution vision analysis, and performance on long-horizon autonomous tasks. A new open-source project called OpenMythos offers a theoretical reconstruction of Anthropic's unpublished Claude Mythos architecture. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/19/meet-openmythos-an-open-source-pytorch-reconstruction-of-claude-mythos-where-770m-parameters-match-a-1-3b-transformer/))

**Moonshot AI and Tsinghua Researchers Propose New LLM Serving Architecture**
A research team from Moonshot AI and Tsinghua University has proposed PrfaaS, a novel cross-datacenter KV-Cache architecture. This new method rethinks how large language models are served at scale by separating the prefill and decode stages across different datacenters, potentially overcoming existing network bottlenecks.
(Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/19/moonshot-ai-and-tsinghua-researchers-propose-prfaas-a-cross-datacenter-kvcache-architecture-that-rethinks-how-llms-are-served-at-scale/))

Meta has developed a "Just-in-Time" (JiT) testing system that dynamically generates tests during code review, rather than relying on static suites. (Source: [InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/04/meta-jit-testing-ai-detection/?utm_campaign=infoq_content&amp;utm_source=infoq&amp;utm_medium=feed&amp;utm_term=AI%2C+ML+%26+Data+Engineering))
