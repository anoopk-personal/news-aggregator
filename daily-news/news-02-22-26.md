## AI News Digest

### Major Model and Platform Releases

**Google Releases Gemini 3.1 Pro with 1M Token Context**
Google has launched Gemini 3.1 Pro, an update focused on agentic AI with improved reasoning and tool-use reliability. The model features a massive 1 million token context window and a 77.1% score on a key reasoning benchmark, positioning it for more complex, multi-step tasks. (Source: MarkTechPost, TechCrunch)

**OpenAI Launches 'Frontier' Enterprise Agent Platform**
OpenAI introduced Frontier, a new platform for enterprises to build, deploy, and manage AI agents at scale. The platform aims to make agents more reliable and deeply integrated into real company systems and workflows, moving beyond simple chatbot interactions. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Databricks Announces Lakebase for AI Workloads**
Databricks has made its Lakebase database generally available, offering a serverless, PostgreSQL-based OLTP database designed for AI workloads. It integrates with the Databricks platform to combine transactional and analytical capabilities with independent scaling. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**NVIDIA Overhauls Dynamo Distributed Inference Framework**
NVIDIA has released Dynamo v0.9.0, a major infrastructure update that simplifies the deployment of large-scale models. Key changes include the removal of heavy dependencies like NATS and etcd and improved support for multi-modal data. (Source: MarkTechPost)

## CRICKET News Digest

### T20 World Cup: Super Eights Underway
**Rain Abandons Opening Super Eight Match**
The first Super Eight fixture between New Zealand and Pakistan in Colombo was washed out without a ball being bowled due to incessant rain. The abandonment means both teams share a point each, adding pressure to their remaining matches in a highly competitive group. (Source: BBC Sport, ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**England and Sri Lanka Face Off in Crucial Clash**
England, facing pressure after an inconsistent group stage, were asked to bat first by co-hosts Sri Lanka in their Super Eight opener in Pallekele. England fielded an unchanged XI for the fourth consecutive game, while Sri Lanka looked to leverage home conditions, despite concerns over a rain-affected outfield. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**India and South Africa Set for High-Stakes Encounter**
Two of the tournament's top contenders, India and South Africa, are preparing for a blockbuster Super Eight match in Ahmedabad, dubbed a "final before the final." South Africa's coach, Shukri Conrad, stated his team's intent to "expose India under pressure" by using the weight of home-crowd expectation against them. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Australia Secures Consolation Win Over Oman**
Already eliminated from the tournament, Australia ended their campaign with a dominant nine-wicket victory against Oman. Spinner Adam Zampa took a four-wicket haul, which he called the "worst-feeling four-for" he's ever had, while Mitchell Marsh guided the chase with an unbeaten 64. (Source: BBC Sport, ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**West Indies Strengthened by Shepherd's Return**
The unbeaten West Indies side has been boosted by the news that all-rounder Romario Shepherd is fit and available for their upcoming Super Eight match against Zimbabwe. Coach Daren Sammy confirmed Shepherd's availability after he recovered from an injury, strengthening the team's prospects. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### The Hundred: Controversy Over Player Selection
**Controversy Surrounds Pakistani Player Participation**
Reports that the four Hundred teams with IPL ownership links will not sign Pakistani players have sparked controversy over a potential "shadow-ban." The ECB has pushed back on these claims, while England's Harry Brook called the situation "a shame," though 67 Pakistani players have still registered for the auction. (Source: BBC Sport, ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### Women's International Cricket
**India Claims Historic Series Win in Australia**
The Indian women's team secured a famous T20I series victory over Australia, handing the hosts their first series loss on home soil since 2017. India won the final match in Adelaide by 17 runs, with Smriti Mandhana, Jemimah Rodrigues, and Shreyanka Patil delivering match-winning performances. (Source: BBC Sport, ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Sri Lanka Edges West Indies; Perry Nears Milestone**
In a tense opening ODI, Sri Lanka's women's team defeated the West Indies by 10 runs, successfully defending 240 thanks to a four-wicket haul from Inoka Ranaweera. Meanwhile, Australian all-rounder Ellyse Perry is set to play her 350th international match, highlighting her remarkable longevity. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### Domestic Cricket Highlights
**Domestic Finals Decided in Australia and New Zealand**
New South Wales powered into Australia's One-Day Cup final on the back of Kurtis Patterson's stunning 125 not out from 89 balls. In New Zealand, Central Districts won the Ford Trophy title, propelled by a record 257-run partnership between Will Young and Curtis Heaphy. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

### Other Cricket News
**BCCI Announces Support for Blind Cricket in India**
The BCCI has formalized its backing for the Cricket Association for the Blind in India (CABI) by announcing a "structured support" framework. This significant move will provide financial aid, access to venues, and support for home and international series, boosting the sport's development in the country. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)

**Maharaj to Captain South Africa in New Zealand**
Keshav Maharaj will lead a new-look South African T20I squad for their upcoming series in New Zealand. Most of the senior players from the T20 World Cup squad are being rested, providing an opportunity for other players to gain international experience. (Source: ESPN Cricinfo.com)
