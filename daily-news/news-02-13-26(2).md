## AI News Digest

### Major Model &amp; Capability Breakthroughs

**Google Unveils Gemini 3 Deep Think for Advanced Scientific Reasoning**
Google has announced a significant update to its Gemini model series with Gemini 3 Deep Think, designed to accelerate science, research, and engineering. The model introduces a "reasoning mode" that uses internal verification to solve complex problems, achieving a state-of-the-art score of 84.6% on the ARC-AGI-2 benchmark, a test of abstract reasoning. This release signals a strategic pivot towards using AI for autonomous research and discovery, moving beyond simple question-answering. (Source: Google AI, MarkTechPost)

**OpenAI Launches GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark for Real-Time Coding**
OpenAI has released a research preview of GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark, a model optimized for extreme speed in code generation. Achieved through a deep hardware-software partnership with chipmaker Cerebras, the model is 15 times faster than its predecessor, capable of generating over 1000 tokens per second. This focus on near-instant response times and a 128k context window aims to make AI a seamless, real-time partner in the software development process. (Source: OpenAI News, MarkTechPost, Ars Technica)

**DeepMind's Aletheia Agent Tackles Professional Mathematical Research**
Google DeepMind introduced Aletheia, a specialized AI agent designed to bridge the gap between solving competition-level math problems and conducting professional research. Building on the success of models that achieved gold-medal standards at the International Mathematical Olympiad, Aletheia can navigate vast scientific literature and construct long-horizon proofs. It works by iteratively generating, verifying, and revising solutions in natural language, demonstrating a path toward fully autonomous research discoveries. (Source: MarkTechPost)

Chinese tech giant Alibaba has unveiled RynnBrain, an open-source multimodal model designed to power physical robots. The model enables robots to perceive their environment and execute complex tasks, marking China's accelerating push into "physical AI." This move is driven by the need to address aging populations and labor shortages with automated, embodied intelligence. (Source: AI News)

**AI Rivals Collaborate on Paris-Based Startup Accelerator**

### The Rise of Agentic AI &amp; Developer Tools

GitHub has launched a technical preview of the GitHub Copilot SDK, which allows developers to integrate the engine behind the Copilot CLI into their own applications. This move is significant as it empowers developers to build custom, agentic workflows that can automate complex development tasks, moving beyond simple code completion to create more autonomous software development tools. (Source: InfoQ)

**Former GitHub CEO Launches 'Entire' for Agentic Coding**
Thomas Dohmke, the former CEO of GitHub, has launched a new developer platform named Entire, specifically designed for the new era of "agentic coding." The platform aims to provide the infrastructure for developers to build, test, and deploy AI agents that can handle complex software engineering tasks autonomously, reflecting a broader industry shift from AI assistants to AI colleagues. **Google Proposes Agentic Framework for Adaptive UI**
Google Research has introduced Natively Adaptive Interfaces (NAI), an agentic framework built on Gemini that allows a multimodal AI agent to serve as the primary user interface. Instead of a fixed UI, the agent adapts the application in real-time to a user's specific abilities and context. This represents a fundamental shift in accessibility design, making software inherently flexible and personalized. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Didero Lands $30M to Automate Manufacturing Procurement with Agentic AI**
Startup Didero has raised $30 million to develop its "agentic autopilot" for manufacturing procurement. The AI system acts as a layer on top of a company's existing ERP software, reading communications and automatically executing tasks like updating orders and coordinating with suppliers. This application demonstrates the practical, high-value use of agentic AI in complex business operations. (Source: TechCrunch)

### AI for Science, Health, and Social Good

**MIT Launches Initiative to Fight Poverty with AI**
MIT's Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL) has launched "Project AI Evidence," a new research and policy initiative to test and scale AI innovations to combat poverty. The project will connect governments and nonprofits with top economists to rigorously evaluate and improve AI solutions in areas like healthcare, education, and social programs, ensuring technology is effectively applied to real-world problems. (Source: MIT News)

**AI and Synthetic Biology Combined to Combat Antimicrobial Resistance**
Researchers at MIT are using a combination of synthetic biology and AI to address the growing global threat of drug-resistant infections. As the development of new antibiotics has slowed, this interdisciplinary approach aims to accelerate the discovery and design of novel antibacterial tools to fight superbugs. (Source: MIT News)

**New AI Algorithm Provides Unprecedented View of Brain's White Matter**
An AI algorithm developed at MIT can now reliably track and resolve distinct nerve bundles (white matter pathways) in the brainstem from live diffusion MRI scans. This tool provides a new, non-invasive window into a critical and complex brain region, enabling researchers and clinicians to better identify signs of injury or diseases like ALS and Parkinson's. (Source: MIT News)

### Hardware, Infrastructure, and Optimization

**NVIDIA Researchers Announce 20x Compression for LLM Memory**
NVIDIA researchers have introduced KVTC, a transform coding pipeline that can compress the Key-Value (KV) cache in large language models by up to 20 times. The KV cache is a major memory bottleneck in LLM serving, and this breakthrough could dramatically improve throughput and latency, making it cheaper and more efficient to deploy large models at scale. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**OpenAI Details Scaling a Single Postgres Database for ChatGPT**
In a technical disclosure, OpenAI explained how it scaled a single-primary PostgreSQL database to handle millions of queries per second for ChatGPT's hundreds of millions of users. The architecture relies on nearly 50 read replicas on Azure, aggressive query optimization, and offloading heavy workloads, providing a case study in managing massive-scale infrastructure for AI services. (Source: InfoQ)

**AI Inference Startup Modal Labs in Talks to Raise at $2.5B Valuation**
Modal Labs, a startup that provides serverless infrastructure for running AI models, is reportedly in talks to raise a new funding round at a $2.5 billion valuation. The high valuation for the four-year-old company highlights the critical importance and market value of AI inference—the process of running trained models—which is often more costly and complex than training itself. (Source: TechCrunch)
