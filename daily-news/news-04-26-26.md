### Advanced Techniques &amp; Developer Focus

**Rethinking RAG: Retrieval by Reasoning, Not Just Vectors**
A new technique called PageIndex is challenging the dominance of vector similarity in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. Instead of finding "closest" matches in a vector space, PageIndex uses reasoning to determine relevance, a method better suited for long, complex professional documents where simple similarity is a poor proxy for correctness.
(Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/25/rag-without-vectors-how-pageindex-retrieves-by-reasoning/))

**The Rise of Hybrid Search for RAG Systems**
Developers are increasingly turning to hybrid search methods for RAG, combining the strengths of lexical search (like BM25) and semantic search (vectors). This approach acknowledges that neither method is perfect; lexical search excels at keywords, while semantic search understands context, and using both is often necessary for robust, production-grade systems.
(Source: [Towards AI - Medium](https://pub.towardsai.net/hybrid-search-for-rag-bm25-vectors-when-each-wins-402f24abaeea?source=rss----98111c9905da---4))

**Beyond Vectors: Tensors Poised to Make AI Search Smarter**
While vector databases have become a cornerstone of modern AI search, the next evolution may involve tensors. Tensors, which are multi-dimensional arrays, can capture more complex relationships and structures within data than one-dimensional vectors, promising to make AI search more nuanced and intelligent.
(Source: [Artificial Intelligence News, Analysis and Resources - The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/vectors-tensors-ai-search-explained/))
