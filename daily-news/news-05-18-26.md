## AI News Digest

### AI Models &amp; Performance
**NVIDIA Introduces a 4-Bit Pretraining Methodology Using NVFP4**
NVIDIA has unveiled a 4-bit pretraining method utilizing the NVFP4 microscaling format. This innovation combines selective BF16 layers, Random Hadamard Transforms, 2D weight scaling, and stochastic rounding on gradients, validated on a 12B hybrid Mamba-Transformer trained on 10 trillion tokens. This represents the longest publicly documented 4-bit pretraining run, achieving downstream accuracy (62.58% on MMLU-Pro) closely matching the FP8 baseline (62.62%), signifying a significant step towards more efficient AI model training. (Source: MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/18/nvidia-introduces-a-4-bit-pretraining-methodology-using-nvfp4-validated-on-a-12b-hybrid-mamba-transformer-at-10t-token-horizon/))

**A Coding Implementation to Compress and Benchmark Instruction-Tuned LLMs with FP8, GPTQ, and SmoothQuant Quantization using llmcompressor**
A tutorial demonstrates how to apply post-training quantization to instruction-tuned language models using `llmcompressor`. It compares various compression strategies like FP8 dynamic quantization, GPTQ W4A16, and SmoothQuant with GPTQ W8A8, benchmarking them for disk size, generation latency, throughput, and perplexity. This is important for optimizing LLM deployment and efficiency. (Source: MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/17/a-coding-implementation-to-compress-and-benchmark-instruction-tuned-llms-with-fp8-gptq-and-smoothquant-quantization-using-llmcompressor/))

**Nous Research Proposes Lighthouse Attention: A Training-Only Selection-Based Hierarchical Attention That Delivers 1.4–1.7× Pretraining Speedup at Long Context**
Nous Research has introduced Lighthouse Attention, a hierarchical attention mechanism that significantly speeds up pretraining. It wraps around standard scaled dot-product attention during pretraining and is removed afterward, reducing attention call complexity from O(N·S·d) to O(S²·d). Tested on a 530M Llama-3-style model at 98K context, it achieved a 1.40–1.69× wall-clock speedup with matching or lower final training loss, offering a notable efficiency boost for long-context models. (Source: MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/16/nous-research-proposes-lighthouse-attention-a-training-only-selection-based-hierarchical-attention-that-delivers-1-4-1-7x-pretraining-speedup-at-long-context/))

**NVIDIA Introduces SANA-WM: A 2.6B-Parameter Open-Source World Model That Generates Minute-Scale 720p Video on a Single GPU**
NVIDIA has released SANA-WM, a 2.6-billion-parameter open-source world model capable of generating 60-second, 720p videos with precise 6-DoF camera control. Trained on 64 H100 GPUs, it can be deployed on a single RTX 5090, showcasing advancements in video generation capabilities and accessibility. (Source: MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/16/nvidia-introduces-sana-wm-a-2-6b-parameter-open-source-world-model-that-generates-minute-scale-720p-video-on-a-single-gpu/))

**Zyphra Releases ZAYA1-8B-Diffusion-Preview: The First MoE Diffusion Model Converted From an Autoregressive LLM With Up to 7.7x Speedup**
Zyphra has demonstrated that an autoregressive Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model can be converted into a discrete diffusion model without systematic loss in evaluation performance. Their ZAYA1-8B-Diffusion-Preview achieves up to 7.7x inference speedup by shifting decoding from memory-bandwidth bound to compute-bound, highlighting a significant improvement in efficiency for diffusion models. (Source: MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/15/zyphra-releases-zaya1-8b-diffusion-preview-the-first-moe-diffusion-model-converted-from-an-autoregressive-llm-with-up-to-7-7x-speedup/))
