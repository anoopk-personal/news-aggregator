## AI News Digest

### AI Models and Development
**Granite Embedding Multilingual R2 Released**
IBM has released Granite Embedding Multilingual R2, open Apache 2.0 multilingual embeddings with 32K context, claiming the best sub-100M retrieval quality. This release is significant for advancing multilingual natural language processing capabilities. (Source: [Hugging Face - Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-granite/granite-embedding-multilingual-r2))

**Nous Research Accelerates LLM Pre-Training with Token Superposition Training**
Nous Research introduced Token Superposition Training (TST), a two-phase pre-training method that can speed up LLM pre-training by up to 2.5x for models ranging from 270M to 10B parameters. This innovation reduces wall-clock training time without altering model architecture, tokenizer, optimizer, or inference behavior, making LLM development more efficient. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/13/nous-research-releases-token-superposition-training-to-speed-up-llm-pre-training-by-up-to-2-5x-across-270m-to-10b-parameter-models/))

**Fastino Labs Open-Sources GLiGuard, a Compact Safety Moderation Model**
Fastino Labs has open-sourced GLiGuard, a 300M parameter safety moderation model that matches or exceeds the accuracy of models 23–90 times its size across nine safety benchmarks. GLiGuard uses an encoder architecture, offering up to 16x higher throughput and 16.6x lower latency than current state-of-the-art models for prompt safety, jailbreak detection, harm classification, and refusal detection. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/13/fastino-labs-open-sources-gliguard-a-300m-parameter-safety-moderation-model-that-matches-or-exceeds-accuracy-of-models-23-90x-its-size/))

**AntAngelMed: A 103B-Parameter Open-Source Medical Language Model**
MedAIBase released AntAngelMed, a 103B-parameter open-source medical language model built on a 1/32 activation-ratio Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture. It activates only 6.1B parameters at inference, achieving performance comparable to 40B dense models while exceeding 200 tokens per second. The model ranks first among open-source models on OpenAI's HealthBench and tops MedAIBench and MedBench leaderboards. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/12/meet-antangelmed-a-103b-parameter-open-source-medical-language-model-built-on-a-1-32-activation-ratio-moe-architecture/))

**Thinking Machines Lab Introduces Interaction Models for Real-Time Human-AI Collaboration**
Mira Murati’s Thinking Machines Lab unveiled a research preview of TML-Interaction-Small, a 276B parameter Mixture-of-Experts model designed for real-time human-AI collaboration. This native multimodal architecture processes audio, video, and text simultaneously in 200ms chunks, eliminating the need for external voice-activity detection and enabling continuous full-duplex exchange. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/13/mira-muratis-thinking-machines-lab-introduces-interaction-models-a-native-multimodal-architecture-for-real-time-human-ai-collaboration/))
