### AI Models &amp; Research

**Hermes Agent Overtakes OpenClaw in Open-Source Rankings**
Nous Research's Hermes Agent, an open-source self-improving AI, has surpassed OpenClaw to claim the top spot on OpenRouter's global daily token rankings. This marks a significant milestone for open-source models, with Hermes generating 224 billion daily tokens compared to OpenClaw's 186 billion, placing it ahead of an OpenAI-sponsored platform in real-world usage. **NVIDIA's 'Star Elastic' Puts Multiple Models in One Checkpoint**
NVIDIA researchers have introduced Star Elastic, a post-training method that embeds multiple reasoning models of different sizes (30B, 23B, and 12B parameters) into a single checkpoint. This technique dramatically reduces training costs and storage needs by eliminating separate training runs and also enables a novel "elastic budget control" for more efficient inference, where a smaller model handles thinking and the full model provides the final answer. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/09/nvidia-ai-releases-star-elastic-one-checkpoint-that-contains-30b-23b-and-12b-reasoning-models-with-zero-shot-slicing/))

**Sakana AI and NVIDIA Speed Up LLMs with New Technique**
Researchers from Sakana AI and NVIDIA have introduced TwELL, a method using L1 regularization and custom CUDA kernels to significantly improve LLM efficiency. By inducing over 99% sparsity in feedforward layers with negligible performance loss, the technique achieves a 20.5% speedup in inference and a 21.9% speedup in training, translating sparsity into real-world throughput gains on GPUs. (Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/11/sakana-ai-and-nvidia-introduce-twell-with-cuda-kernels-for-20-5-inference-and-21-9-training-speedup-in-llms/))
