<h3>Technical Breakthroughs &amp; Research</h3>
<p><strong>NVIDIA Introduces KVTC to Compress LLM Caches by 20x</strong>
NVIDIA researchers have developed KVTC (Key-Value Transform Coding), a pipeline that can compress the Key-Value (KV) cache in large language models by a factor of 20. The KV cache is a major memory bottleneck in LLM serving, and this breakthrough could significantly improve throughput, reduce latency, and lower the hardware costs associated with deploying large-scale models. (Source: MarkTechPost)</p>
<p><strong>Microsoft Proposes OrbitalBrain for AI in Space</strong>
Microsoft researchers have introduced &quot;OrbitalBrain,&quot; a framework for distributed machine learning in space that uses inter-satellite communication links. The system is designed to overcome the severe bandwidth bottleneck of downloading massive datasets from Earth observation satellites, enabling model training to occur directly in orbit. This could revolutionize real-time analysis of satellite imagery for applications like climate monitoring and disaster response. (Source: MarkTechPost)</p>
<p><strong>MIT Researchers Use AI to Accelerate Scientific Discovery</strong>
Researchers at MIT are at an &quot;inflection point&quot; in using AI and simulations to dramatically speed up scientific discovery, according to Associate Professor Rafael Gómez-Bombarelli. Other MIT projects are applying AI to tackle global antimicrobial resistance by identifying new antibacterial compounds and developing algorithms to finely resolve and track vital white matter pathways in the brainstem from live MRI scans, opening new windows into injury and disease. (Source: MIT News - Artificial intelligence)</p>
<p><strong>New Technique Enables Privacy-Preserving LLM Fine-Tuning</strong>
A new method demonstrates how to use federated learning to fine-tune large language models with techniques like LoRA without centralizing private data. By combining the Flower federated learning framework with PEFT (Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning), organizations can collaboratively adapt a shared base model by exchanging only lightweight adapter parameters, ensuring sensitive training data remains on local servers. (Source: MarkTechPost)</p>
