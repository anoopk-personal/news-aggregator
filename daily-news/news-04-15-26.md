## AI News Digest

### AI Models &amp; Corporate Strategy

**Anthropic Unveils Flagship 'Mythos' Model with Gated Release**
Anthropic has announced Claude Mythos Preview, a new flagship model representing a significant leap in capability, particularly in reasoning and cybersecurity. Citing its power to find and exploit software vulnerabilities, Anthropic has forgone a public release, instead limiting access to "Project Glasswing," a consortium of over 40 critical infrastructure companies like Google, Microsoft, and Apple. The move highlights a new era of "gated" releases for potentially dangerous AI, as the company has already briefed government bodies, including the Trump administration, on the model's capabilities. (Source: Towards AI, InfoQ, AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**Google Launches "Skills" to Automate AI Workflows in Chrome**
Google is rolling out "Skills" for its Gemini integration in the Chrome browser, allowing users to save and reuse frequently used AI prompts as one-click tools. The feature, available for English-US users on Mac, Windows, and ChromeOS, aims to streamline repetitive tasks by turning complex prompts into simple, shareable workflows accessible from the Gemini sidebar. This move deepens AI integration into the browser, transforming it from a simple information retriever into a more active work assistant. (Source: MarkTechPost, AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch, AI, Feed: Artificial Intelligence Latest, The New Stack)

**Anthropic's Rise Creates Market Ripples and Policy Clashes**
Anthropic's rapid ascent is causing some OpenAI investors to have second thoughts, with one noting that Anthropic's valuation seems like a relative bargain compared to the massive figures needed to justify OpenAI's recent funding rounds. The company is also taking distinct policy stances, publicly opposing an Illinois liability bill that OpenAI supported, which would have limited lab responsibility for AI-caused disasters. This divergence in strategy and policy highlights the growing competition and differing philosophies among top AI labs. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch, Feed: Artificial Intelligence Latest)

**OpenAI Acquires Personal Finance Startup Hiro**
OpenAI has acquired Hiro, a startup specializing in AI-powered personal finance tools. The acquisition signals OpenAI's intent to build more sophisticated financial planning and analysis capabilities directly into its platforms like ChatGPT. This move could position OpenAI to compete in the fintech space by offering users personalized financial advice and management through its conversational AI. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

**Vercel Signals IPO Readiness as AI Agent Development Fuels Growth**
Vercel, a popular web development and hosting platform, is benefiting from the explosion in AI-powered applications and agents, with CEO Guillermo Rauch signaling the company is preparing for a potential IPO. The platform's infrastructure is proving crucial for developers building and deploying new AI-driven tools, leading to a significant surge in revenue and positioning Vercel as a key player in the AI ecosystem's underlying infrastructure. (Source: AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

### New Models &amp; Research Breakthroughs

**Google Releases Gemma 4 and Upgrades Gemini for Robotics**
Google has released Gemma 4, a new family of open models focused on enabling local, on-device AI inference, particularly for Android development. Concurrently, Google DeepMind launched Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6, an enhanced model designed to be the "cognitive brain" for physical robots, improving their embodied reasoning, spatial understanding, and ability to read instruments in real-world environments. These releases underscore Google's dual strategy of pushing both on-device and large-scale physical AI. (Source: InfoQ, MarkTechPost)

**NVIDIA and University of Maryland Release Open Audio-Language Model**
Researchers from NVIDIA and the University of Maryland have introduced Audio Flamingo Next (AF-Next), a powerful, open-source large audio-language model. The model aims to advance the frontier of multimodal AI beyond vision by enabling robust reasoning over various audio types, including speech, music, and environmental sounds. This open release provides the research community with a strong foundation for building more sophisticated audio-understanding applications. (Source: MarkTechPost)

**Claude Code Discovers 23-Year-Old Linux Kernel Vulnerability**
In a powerful demonstration of AI's cybersecurity potential, an Anthropic researcher used Claude Code to discover a remotely exploitable heap buffer overflow vulnerability in the Linux kernel's NFS driver that had remained hidden for 23 years. This event is part of a larger trend where AI-generated bug reports are shifting from "slop" to legitimate findings, with kernel maintainers now receiving multiple valid AI-discovered vulnerability reports daily. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Anthropic Research Investigates Emotion-Like Mechanisms in LLMs**
A new research paper from Anthropic's interpretability team examines how models like Claude Sonnet 4.5 internally represent concepts related to emotions and how these representations influence their behavior. By analyzing the model's internal activations, the research seeks to understand the mechanisms behind model responses, contributing to the broader effort of making powerful AI systems more transparent and predictable. (Source: InfoQ - AI, ML &amp; Data Engineering)

**Google Proposes "Vantage" to Measure Human Skills with LLMs**
Google AI Research has proposed Vantage, a new protocol that uses LLMs to measure "durable skills" like collaboration, creativity, and critical thinking, which are difficult to assess with standardized tests. The system aims to provide a more holistic evaluation of a person's abilities by analyzing their performance in complex, simulated scenarios, potentially transforming educational and professional assessments. (Source: MarkTechPost)

The 2026 Stanford AI Index Report highlights several worrying trends, including the closing performance gap between US and Chinese AI models, while the gap in responsible AI practices widens. The comprehensive report also notes a growing disconnect between AI insiders and the general public, with rising public anxiety over AI's impact on jobs, healthcare, and the economy. (Source: AI News, AI News &amp; Artificial Intelligence | TechCrunch)

(Source: Feed: Artificial Intelligence Latest)

**Security Concerns Mount Around Edge AI and Autonomous Agents**
The rise of powerful, on-device models like Google's Gemma 4 is creating new governance challenges for enterprise security teams. As AI workloads move to the "edge," traditional cloud-centric security measures are becoming insufficient. Simultaneously, the increasing autonomy of AI agents is raising alarms about a new class of security nightmares if these agents are compromised or act maliciously. (Source: AI News, KDnuggets)
